# Animation 04 — `nano` — Nano's ANOther editor

## Chapter 6 — Text Editors

## Source animation sequence

The word `nano` appears.

It opens.

Inside is another smaller `nano`.

That opens.

Inside is another.

And another.

The camera keeps zooming inward until the letters become microscopic.

A tiny footer appears at the bottom:

`^X Exit`

The camera zooms another ten levels to make it readable.

A large human finger enters frame, misses the tiny `^X`, and presses the entire universe instead.

## Sequence lock

**Guide tabs:** `NANO` / `ANOTHER EDITOR`

**Palette:** mustard, deep cobalt, violet, coral red, pale cyan

**Total duration:** 15 seconds (3 × 5-second cuts)

- Use only 4–7 matte colors in the scene. No gradients. No gloss. No bloom. No drop shadows. No translucent glass. No depth-of-field. Very light imperfect print/screen texture is welcome; edges may feel slightly handmade.
- People, aliens, machines, files and processes are simplified geometric silhouettes and cutout shapes with deliberately economical anatomy. Minimal facial detail. Express ideas through pose, scale, labels and objects rather than acting or lip-sync.
- Favor bold asymmetric composition and large areas of empty color. Perspective is flattened. Diagrams may become rooms; labels may become physical objects; metaphors are treated as literal information graphics.
- Motion is crisp, graphic and primarily linear: slides, pivots, hinges, flips, rotations, wipes, mechanical relabeling, 2D pans and zooms. No camera shake. No photorealistic physics. No dissolves or soft fades. Avoid bouncy modern easing; changes should feel diagrammatic and inevitable.
- Comedy must be dry. Nobody performs the joke for camera. The visual system explains a technically correct thing, follows one innocent word too literally, and lets the consequence become absurd.
- No generic outer-space imagery unless the beat specifically requires it. No neon cyberpunk, holograms, glossy app UI, anime, 3D cartoon rendering, Pixar-like character animation, cinematic lens effects, or realistic terminal windows.
- Do not invent extra dialogue or labels. For text longer than a few words, reserve a clean sign/card area and composite the exact typography in post if the video model cannot render it reliably.
