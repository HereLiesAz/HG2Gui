# Cat tangent — image prompts

22 boundary frames for the 21 cuts of `23_cat_theological_mechanical_history`. Frame `NN` is the START of cut `NN+1`; the same image is the END of cut `NN`. Frame 21 is the end of the last cut.

Frames 00–11 are clean redraws of the existing stills `cat_01.png`–`cat_12.png` (those are tiny, wide and carry a burned-in `Start:` label). Frames 12–21 are new, for the passage added after the original.

## How to use

1. Generate the character reference once and keep it.
2. Generate frames in order. For every frame attach the character reference and the previous approved frame, and say "keep the cat exactly as in the references".
3. For frames 00–11 also attach the original still named in the heading. It sets the composition; do not copy its label.
4. Paste the **style block**, then the frame prompt. The frame prompts never mention files, cut numbers or production terms; keep it that way.

## Style block (paste before every frame prompt)

~~~text
Square image, 1:1. One single flat background colour fills the whole square edge to edge. Flat 2D screen-printed cut-paper pictogram style: simple opaque cutout shapes, economical geometric silhouettes, matte colour, slightly imperfect printed edges, large areas of empty space, asymmetrical placement. No gradients, no gloss, no drop shadows, no 3D, no photographic detail. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no border, no frame, no header. Deadpan, calm, absurd situations shown with complete seriousness.

The cat is always the same character: a flat cream-coloured cutout with a round head, two small triangular ears, thin cream whiskers, tiny dark dot eyes and a small dark nose. Dark ink is navy-black. Accent colours are brick red and mustard.
~~~

## Notes

- Frames 12–21 are new compositions chosen from the narration. Reword any of them freely; the chain only needs each frame to be one calm, stable picture.
- No frame contains readable text. The Persian cuneiform (frame 13) and the Egyptian glyphs are decorative marks that carry no message, so the generator has nothing to misspell.
- The original `cat_NN.png` stills stay in `docs/` untouched. The new images, once approved, would replace the labelled starts as the boundary frames.
