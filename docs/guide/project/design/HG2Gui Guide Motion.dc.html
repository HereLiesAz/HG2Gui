HG2Gui — motion
Text arrives in layers

A Guide entry is not a page that appears — it is assembled in front of you, flat and mechanical, in reading order. Nothing fades and nothing eases into place: pieces wipe on, slide along one axis, and stop dead. Depth is carried by speed alone, the way flat cut-outs on separate planes betray their distance when the camera moves.

The left screen plays itself. The right one is driven by your scroll — drag inside it and watch the three planes separate.

The motion rules this adds

Three planes, and depth is speed: the wash word behind moves at a fifth of the scroll, the coloured rods at half, the text at full. Nothing is blurred or dimmed to fake distance — flat things at different speeds is the whole trick. Every piece wipes on along one axis and stops dead, in reading order, one after another rather than all at once. A line of text is revealed whole; letters are never animated apart, because a word you cannot read yet is not typography. Entries play once and settle — replay is a button, never a loop.
