# A SMALL EMPIRE OF DEPENDENCIES

**Placement:** immediately before the canonical `pkg install` dependency-intervention sequence  
**Duration:** 60 seconds — 12 × 5-second cuts

The term **package manager** comes from the Poffelian profession of managing packages.

This became necessary when packages were granted the right to arrive with anything they depended upon.

At first, this meant string.

Then labels.

Then other packages.

The other packages naturally possessed dependencies of their own.

Warehouses became factories.

Factories acquired supply chains.

Supply chains acquired relationships, several of which were described as essential by people who had shares in them.

Balance was introduced as a theoretical objective.

The original package remained somewhere in the resulting economy and was eventually located by a specialist search department.

Modern package managers have improved this procedure mainly by performing it inside a computer.

When you ask `pkg install` to install a package, the package manager resolves the packages it depends upon and installs the required dependencies as well.

This is considered success because the alternative requires another ministry.
