# CAT — A Brief Theological and Mechanical History

**Placement:** immediately before the canonical `cat` — concatenate sequence  
**Duration:** 60 seconds — 12 × 5-second cuts

The word `cat` entered computing through a disagreement over which way up a cat goes.

This was not initially considered a difficult question.

Cats corrected the record.

A cat regards whichever surface it presently occupies as the upper surface. Turning the cat over merely causes the universe to acquire a new upper surface.

The Egyptians discovered this early and, lacking a standards committee, mistook standardization for divinity.

The principle spread.

Physicists tested gravity against it. Gravity continued to operate, but was forced to admit that the cat had declined to participate in the discussion.

Further studies established that cats prefer darkness, regard food selection as constitutional law, and recognize hierarchy only when personally occupying its highest available point.

Computing eventually shortened **concatenate** to `cat`.

This was convenient because the word was already accustomed to joining a great many unrelated things under a single heading.

In Unix, `cat` writes the contents of its file arguments to standard output in sequence. With one file, it writes that file.

The theological consequences are implementation-defined.
